Privacy Policy

AN-NUR Quran (com.annur.quran)

Effective Date: October 2026

AN-NUR ("we", "our", or "the app") is a dedicated, 100% offline, ad-free 15-line Hafezi Quran reader application designed to provide a peaceful, focused, and distraction-free recitation experience. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information Collection and Use

AN-NUR does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

  • No Account Required: No registration, login, or user credentials are needed to use any feature of this app.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not integrate any third-party analytics, crash-reporting libraries, or user tracking SDKs.
  • No Advertising: The app is 100% ad-free and contains no advertisement networks (e.g., AdMob).
  • No Internet Communication: The app operates completely offline. No data is transmitted to any external server or API.

2. Device Permissions

AN-NUR only uses device permissions that are strictly necessary to deliver local on-device reminder features requested by the user:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ solely to deliver scheduled daily Quran reading reminders created by you.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & USE_EXACT_ALARM: Used strictly to trigger scheduled daily reminder alarms at the exact user-specified time.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used solely to reschedule your active reminder alarms if your device is restarted.
  • VIBRATE & WAKE_LOCK: Used to alert you with vibration when a scheduled reminder is due.

We do not request or access sensitive permissions such as Internet access, precise/approximate location, camera, microphone, contacts, or shared external storage.

3. Local Data Storage

All user preferences—including bookmarks, Tadabbur notes, display themes, brightness settings, and your last-read page—are stored exclusively on your local device using Android DataStore. This information never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing the application data or uninstalling the application.

4. Third-Party Services

The application does not use any third-party services that collect, track, or monitor your identity, behavior, or device information.

5. Children's Privacy

Because AN-NUR does not collect any personal data whatsoever, it is completely safe for users of all ages, including children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated effective date.
