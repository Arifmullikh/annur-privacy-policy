Android System Permissions

An-Nur only requests device permissions that are strictly necessary to deliver essential spiritual features. Here is complete transparency on how every permission is used:

📍

Location (Fine & Coarse) ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION

Used strictly while using the app (foreground) to calculate astronomical prayer times and determine the precise Qibla compass bearing to Makkah. Coordinates are never logged or stored on remote servers.

Foreground Only
🔔

Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Allows delivering on-device prayer alarms (Adhan), Tahajjud alerts, Daily Ayah reflections, and fasting reminders. All notifications are scheduled locally via Android AlarmManager without remote pushes.

Local Alarms
⏰

Schedule Exact Alarms SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM

Ensures the Azan and Sahur alarms trigger at the exact minute required by Islamic law, even when your phone is in deep sleep (Doze mode).

Precision Adhan
🔒

Optional biometric lock for your private Quran Journal and notes. Verified 100% locally by Android's hardware security module; the app never accesses raw biometric data.

Hardware Locked
🎧

Enables seamless Quran recitation audio playback while your screen is off or while you are multitasking in other apps.

Data We Collect & How It Is Used

Because An-Nur is built with an offline-first architecture, the vast majority of your data lives exclusively inside your phone's private storage directory.

  • Local Reading Data: Bookmarks, reading history, Khatam progress, Ayah notes, and Tasbih counter records are stored on your local SQLite database.
  • Audio Caching: Downloaded Surah recitations are kept in your app-specific cache directory for offline listening.
  • Diagnostic Logs: If an unexpected crash occurs, anonymous technical crash reports (device model, Android OS version, stack trace) may be collected to help us patch bugs. These logs contain no personal identifiers.
4

Location Handling & Qibla Direction

Computing accurate prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and the Qibla bearing requires geographic coordinates (Latitude and Longitude).

📌 Zero Background Tracking: Location coordinates are obtained only when you actively trigger prayer time configuration or open the Qibla Compass screen. When using the Aladhan prayer times service, coordinates are rounded (~100m) to preserve your exact privacy. Nearby mosque searches query open data from OpenStreetMap.

Optional Account & Cloud Sync

You do not need an account to use 100% of An-Nur's reading and worship features. However, for users who wish to synchronize their bookmarks and notes across multiple devices:

We offer optional authentication via Google Sign-In powered by secure, modern cloud infrastructure (Firebase Auth / Supabase). When enabled, only your user ID, email address, and synced Quran bookmarks/notes are stored securely in encrypted cloud tables.

Children's Privacy & Family Safety

An-Nur complies fully with the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

The application is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app has no ads, no social feeds, and no unmoderated communication channels, parents can trust An-Nur as a safe educational tool for their entire family.

Your Rights & Data Deletion

Under GDPR, CCPA, and Google Play Store policies, you have complete sovereignty over your data:

  • Instant In-App Deletion: You can delete your cloud-synced account and associated records directly inside the app under Settings → Account & Cloud Sync → Delete Account & Cloud Data.
  • Uninstalling the App: Since local data is stored within the app's sandboxed storage, uninstalling the app permanently purges all on-device notes, bookmarks, and cached files.
  • Web Deletion Request: Visit our dedicated Account Deletion Request Page to view web-based deletion instructions.
  • Manual Removal Request: You may email us directly at arifmullikh.personal@gmail.com with the subject "An-Nur Account Deletion Request" to request permanent deletion of any synced cloud records. We process all requests within 48 hours.
