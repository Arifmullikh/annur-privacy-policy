Privacy Policy

AN-NUR Quran (com.annur.quran)

Last updated: October 25, 2026

AN-NUR ("we", "our", or "the app") is an ad-free 15-line Hafezi Quran reader with recitation audio, prayer times, Adhan, Qibla and other Islamic features. The Quran pages are stored inside the app and can be read fully offline. Some features use the internet, as explained below. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

AN-NUR does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share your personal information.

  • No Account Required: There is no registration or login.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.
  • No Advertising: The app is 100% ad-free and contains no ad networks.

2. Location

If you allow location access, the app uses your device's location only to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. To get accurate prayer times, your approximate coordinates (latitude and longitude) are sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to the Aladhan prayer-times service (api.aladhan.com). The coordinates are used only to return prayer times; we do not store them on any server or use them for any other purpose. Location is optional: you can deny the permission and the rest of the app keeps working.

3. Internet Features

The app connects to the following public services only to download content you request. No personal information is sent to them:

  • Quran recitation audio (everyayah.com, api.quran.com)
  • Quran text and translations (api.quran.com, api.alquran.cloud)
  • Hadith collections (cdn.jsdelivr.net)
  • Adhan audio (cdn.aladhan.com)
  • Live Makkah and Madinah broadcasts (Saudi Broadcasting Authority streams)
  • Ruqyah audio (islamicapi.com)

These services may receive standard technical information such as your IP address, as with any internet connection. Their own privacy policies apply to that information.

Links for feedback (Google Forms), WhatsApp, Google Maps (nearby mosques) and Google Play open in your browser or the related app and are governed by those services' policies.

4. Device Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To download recitation audio, translations, hadith and prayer times, and play live streams.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Optional, for prayer times and Qibla direction.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: For Adhan, prayer and reading reminders, and the audio player notification.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: To play the Adhan and reminders at the exact time.
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT: To show the Adhan screen at prayer time, only if you enable it.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: To keep Quran recitation and Adhan playing in the background.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: To restore your alarms after the device restarts.
  • VIBRATE & WAKE_LOCK: For alerts and uninterrupted playback.

The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or files.

5. Local Data Storage

Your bookmarks, notes, settings, last-read page, downloaded audio and cached prayer times are stored only on your device. You can delete them anytime by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

6. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
