Request deletion of your An-Nur account

If you can open An-Nur, use Settings → Account & Sync → Delete account. The app will ask you to sign in again, remove the Firebase account and its synced An-Nur records, and keep your local device data unchanged.

If you cannot access the app, email the developer from the address used for your An-Nur account and request account deletion. Do not include your password or sign-in tokens.

Email an account deletion request

The developer may ask you to verify account ownership before processing a request. A deletion request removes your Firebase account and synced records. It does not erase records or backups saved only on your device or in a local backup file; clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove local data.

This page must be published at a stable HTTPS address and monitored by the app developer before it is submitted as the Play Store account deletion URL.
