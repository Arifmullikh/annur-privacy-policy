Request An-Nur Account Deletion

Google Play Store 2026 Compliant Deletion Portal

We respect your privacy and provide simple mechanisms to delete your cloud-synchronized An-Nur account and all associated cloud data at any time.

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

  1. Open the An-Nur app on your Android device.
  2. Go to Settings → Account & Cloud Sync.
  3. Tap Delete Account & Cloud Data and confirm.

Your cloud authentication profile, stored sync tokens, and cloud bookmarks will be permanently erased immediately.

Method 2: Email Deletion Request (Within 48h)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, email us directly from the address associated with your Google / An-Nur account.

✉️ Submit Deletion Request via Email
Data Retention Policy: When account deletion is completed, all cloud-synced Quran bookmarks, notes, and profile records are permanently removed from our Firebase/Supabase database. Local on-device data remains on your physical phone; you can erase all local data simply by clearing the app data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.
